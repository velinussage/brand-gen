# Principles

## Doctrine
- Lead with crisp product proof and keep decorative atmosphere secondary.
- Build confidence through whitespace, pacing, and calm editorial hierarchy.
- Use one convincing showcase move per frame instead of equal-weight mosaics.

## Constraints
- Keep supporting surfaces subordinate to the main product carrier.
- Prefer restraint over visual chatter, crowded proof rows, or ornamental chrome.

## Anti-patterns
- Avoid collage layouts, equal-weight panels, or too many simultaneous focal points.
- Avoid generic gradients, novelty effects, or decorative chrome that weakens trust.
- Avoid foreign logos, literal copy, or source-specific brand assets in generated work.
